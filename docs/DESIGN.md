# Design system

- **Display:** 240 × 135 pixels, RGB565.
- **Type:** Inter for Latin text and numerals; Noto Sans SC for Simplified Chinese.
- **Clock:** natural-width bold time numerals, an orange colon, compact date/status rows and stable status slots.
- **Menus:** two square category icons per page, a white focus ring with category-colour glow, four short pagination segments.
- **Selectors:** centred option symbols; Bluetooth channels retain numbered badges.
- **Controls:** centred value and segmented scale for Brightness/Speed; circular colour swatches; quieter/louder action feedback for Volume.
- **Feedback:** centred pairing, connected and timeout symbols without redundant screen text.
- **Boot:** an unbranded orange-colon reveal into the clock.

White is primary content; grey is secondary. Orange is the clock accent, yellow indicates Caps Lock, red marks low battery and green marks charging. Category colours distinguish main-menu icons. State does not depend on colour alone: the selected item also has a ring, and status icons have distinct shapes.

Inspect native PNGs at 1× as well as integer enlargement. A larger browser preview cannot increase the physical display's resolution. Some compact readouts are necessarily smaller than the general label size.

Lighting thumbnails are illustrative visual cues. Native mode IDs and vendor names, rather than the thumbnail appearance, define the selected effect.
